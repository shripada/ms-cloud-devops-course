Run the containers you built in module 1 on a cluster that heals, scales and updates them for you. Practice runs on a three-node cluster on your laptop, with one control plane node and two worker nodes, like a real company cluster. Use Docker Desktop's built-in Kubernetes (kind type) or minikube.
